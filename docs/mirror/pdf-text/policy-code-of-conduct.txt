Success at Pharos Academy depends on the partnership between student, parent,
teachers and Head of School. We require our Pharos Academy parents and students to
carefully review this Code of Conduct. The expectations and consequences stated below
are required at Pharos Academy. Upon completion of reading this document, we ask that
both parent and student sign this form. Thank you in advance for your cooperation.
Code of Conduct
Pharos Academy is intended to provide instruction to self-disciplined young men and
women. Students must be prepared, attentive, and free from distraction to benefit from
this type of educational opportunity. All students are expected to conduct themselves in
accordance with biblical standards and in a manner which will not be distracting to other
students (See Below).
“Only let your conduct be worthy of the gospel of Christ.” Philippians 1:27
STUDENTS:
• Students who participate in Pharos Academy will act in such a way that their words
and actions promote the unity and encouragement of fellow Pharos Academy
students.
• Students will avoid acting in such a way that is considered disrespectful, oJensive,
immoral, intimidating or bullying.
• Students and parents must dress modestly adhering to the Dress Policy set forth in
the Pharos Academy Handbook. Students who arrive at Pharos Academy dressed
inappropriately (in judgment of any Pharos Academy instructor or Head of School)
will be sent home or will be provided with appropriate clothing.
• Students will not gossip to or about other students or families.
• Students must respect the property of others by not touching anything that does not
belong to them unless they have permission.
• Students must respect the church property.
• Students are expected to complete all assignments thoroughly and on time.
• Students must be in their seats and prepared before the start of the class period.
They may not wait for their teacher to call them to class.
• Poor or disrespectful attitudes towards instructors are grounds for dismissal
without refunds.
• The student must respect the individual teacher’s classroom cellphone policy. Refer
to the Pharos Academy Handbook for the general cellphone use policy.

ENOLA FIRST CHURCH OF GOD FACILITY:
• Enola First Church of God has ministry functions throughout the week and an active
church oJice; therefore, we must be respectful while on the church campus.
• Students may not run in the building or hallways. The halls must be kept clear and
quiet.
• Eating and drinking is only allowed in the designated social area, except bottled
water. All trash must be picked up and thrown away immediately before leaving the
social area.
• Students may not use the church’s kitchen, refrigerator, microwave or supplies.
• Students may not sit or climb on the tables or counters.
• Bathrooms should not be used to congregate; they should be left clean and in order.
• Any damage caused by a student to the building, tables, etc. is the financial
responsibility of that student and his/her family. (Defacing walls, tables, buildings,
etc.)
• Students may not go into any part of the building other than the assigned
classrooms and specially designated areas.
Disciplinary Action
First Infraction - verbal warning by Teacher, Head of School or Spiritual Advisor and an
Infraction form will be sent home and placed in student’s file.
Second Infraction -removal from class and an immediate phone call to the parent.
Student will sit with the Head of School or designated staJ member until parent can come
pick up their student. An Infraction form will be sent home and placed in student’s file. A
parent conference with the Teacher and/or Head of School is required before student is
allowed to return to class.
Third Infraction - Removal from the Pharos Academy program for the remainder of the
school year.
Students who do not abide by the Pharos Academy Code of Conduct and have three
Infractions based on the Disciplinary Actions listed above will result in the students
being dismissed from the Pharos Academy program without refund.
